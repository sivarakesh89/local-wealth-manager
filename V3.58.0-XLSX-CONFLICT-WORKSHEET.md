# FinMate v3.58.0 — XLSX Conflict Resolution Worksheet

## What's new

The optional conflict-resolution worksheet from the three-way review is now exported as `FinMate-Conflict-Resolution-Worksheet.xlsx` instead of JSON. The workbook contains:

- **Summary** — source review, baseline/vault identifiers, pending count, and safety notice.
- **Decisions** — one row per changed record, with classification, local/Drive presence, changed flags, an initial `UNRESOLVED` decision, allowed decision values, and a notes column. The table has an autofilter and practical column widths.
- **Instructions** — human review and privacy guidance.

## Safety limits

- Exporting or editing the workbook does not change FinMate local records, Google Drive vault, shared baseline, media, or the pending-change journal.
- The workbook is a review artifact, not a vault backup, and cannot be imported to apply decisions.
- The worksheet may become stale; re-run three-way review before relying on it.
- It contains record IDs and labels. Keep it private.
- Excel export uses the existing SheetJS library loaded by the app. If the library is unavailable, the export fails visibly and does not change data.

## Staging tests

1. Deploy to staging and confirm About shows `3.58.0`.
2. Run Three-way conflict review with disposable test records and choose to create a worksheet.
3. Confirm an `.xlsx` file downloads and opens in Excel or LibreOffice.
4. Confirm the workbook has Summary, Decisions, and Instructions sheets.
5. Confirm each Decision starts as `UNRESOLVED`, allowed choices and notes are present, and the Decisions sheet can be filtered.
6. Edit a decision cell in the downloaded workbook and confirm no FinMate data changes.
7. Confirm local records, Drive vault, baseline, media, and pending-change count remain unchanged.
8. Re-run the review and verify the workbook reflects the newest review.

Syntax and ZIP integrity checks do not replace live browser/Google Drive staging tests.
