# FinMate v3.41.0 changelog

- Google Drive upload success feedback is no longer delayed by downloading the entire uploaded vault for verification. Upload success is reported after the Drive upload and local persistence complete; lightweight Drive metadata verification runs in the background.
- Gold page now displays the latest fetched Goodreturns Bengaluru 22K and 24K rates beneath the fetch button, and remembers the displayed rates in the encrypted app state. Fetching rates works even when no jewellery entries have been added.
- Your jewellery and Other jewellery dropdown summaries now show item count, total metal grams, total invested/purchase amount, and current value.
- Your properties now supports sorting by newest or oldest purchase date; entries without a purchase date remain after dated entries.
- Recurring entries use the same expense-category dropdown as transactions, and now include Tags and Notes. Recurring Type offers only Expense and Income. Auto-created transactions carry the recurring entry's tags and notes.

Existing IndexedDB vault database and encrypted vault format are unchanged.
