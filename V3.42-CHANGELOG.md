# FinMate v3.42.0

## Gold analytics and yearly summary filters
- Added independent jewellery scope selectors to Gold Analytics and Yearly Summary: Your jewellery, Other jewellery, and Both jewellery.
- Analytics now recalculates item count, metal weight, investment, current value, and weight/value breakdowns based on its selected scope.
- Yearly Summary recalculates grams, item count, investment, and current value based on its selected scope.
- Each selector remembers its current choice during rerenders in the current app session.
- Preserved the existing IndexedDB database name, encrypted vault format, and Google Drive sync flow.
