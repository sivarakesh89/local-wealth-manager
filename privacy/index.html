Privacy at a glance: FinMate is designed to keep financial records in an encrypted vault in your browser/device. Google Drive synchronization is optional. When enabled, FinMate uploads the encrypted vault—not a readable transaction ledger—to the user's own Google Drive.

1. Scope and operator

This policy describes the FinMate personal finance and wealth-management Progressive Web App (PWA), including its local storage, optional synchronization, imports, and market-data features. The application is currently provided by its developer. The developer's contact email must be configured in the public release before distribution.

2. Information you enter

Depending on which features you use, information can include assets and investments, balances, transactions, income and expenses, budgets, goals, liabilities, property and jewellery records, notes, photos, bills, documents, and financial profile details. You should not enter bank login passwords, card PINs, UPI PINs, OTPs, or other authentication secrets into FinMate.

3. Where information is stored

FinMate is designed to store your records in browser-local storage (including IndexedDB) in an encrypted vault. Local data is associated with the browser origin and profile/device where it was created. Moving to a different URL, browser, device, or browser profile does not automatically move that data. Keep a secure backup and verify restoration before changing installations.

4. Encryption and password responsibility

The application uses browser cryptography to encrypt the vault before persistence or synchronization. Your master password is used to protect access to the vault key. A PIN or device biometric, where enabled and supported, is intended as a convenience unlock method on that device. These controls do not remove the need to keep your master password and recovery backups safe. Anyone who obtains both an encrypted backup and the credentials needed to decrypt it may be able to access its contents.

5. Optional Google Drive synchronization

If you choose to connect Google Drive, FinMate uses Google OAuth to obtain permission for the Drive operations described in the consent screen. The encrypted vault is stored in your own Google Drive account. The OAuth Worker exchanges authorization codes and tokens; it is not intended to receive the plaintext financial vault. Google processes account and authorization information under Google's own policies. You can disconnect synchronization from FinMate and revoke access in your Google Account settings.

6. Third-party services and market data

Depending on features enabled, the app may load Google's sign-in/API scripts, a spreadsheet parsing library from a content-delivery network, and external market-data endpoints or a proxy you configure. Requests to those services are subject to their own terms and privacy policies. Market-data requests may reveal technical request information such as IP address to the provider. Do not enable a price proxy unless you trust its operator.

7. Analytics, advertising and sale of data

FinMate is not designed to sell users' financial records or to provide those records to advertisers. This statement does not mean that every third-party script or infrastructure provider is incapable of receiving technical connection information. If analytics, advertising, telemetry, or additional backend services are added in a future version, this policy should be updated before those features are released.

8. Sharing and disclosure

FinMate's local-first design does not create a central developer database of your vault. You control any exports, screenshots, backups, or files you choose to share. Information may also be processed by Google or other providers when you use their integrations, as described above.

9. Retention and deletion

Your local records remain until you delete them, clear the browser's site data, remove the relevant browser profile, or otherwise lose the local storage. Clearing site data may permanently remove the vault. Drive copies remain in your Drive until you delete them. Disconnecting OAuth access does not necessarily delete files already stored in Drive.

10. Children's information

FinMate is intended for use by an adult managing personal or family finances. It is not designed to collect personal information directly from children.

11. Security limitations

No software, browser, device, backup, or internet transmission can be guaranteed perfectly secure. Keep your devices updated, use a strong master password, protect backups, and avoid shared/public devices. Report suspected security issues using the developer contact link.

12. Policy changes

This policy may be updated as FinMate features or services change. The version published at this URL applies from its stated publication/update date. Significant changes should be reflected in the release notes.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or data-related requests, use the Contact link above. The developer must configure a valid contact email before the public release.
